How these rankings work +

A 2,000g build that adds 20 damage per second scores +10 DPS per 1,000g. Gain is measured against the same hero with no items. Each build belongs to one spending range and must improve on cheaper choices for that hero.

A Legend role board uses heroes assigned to that position in eligible replays. Patch membership may be inferred from match start time. The full roster is a mechanics sandbox.

Magic uses one cast of the strongest supported learned spell and modeled item damage during the selected window. The separate item-only results use zero spell damage for heroes whose spell effects are outside the model.

Ability damage ranks gains per cast. Omnislash projects whole hits against one stationary target, starting with an immediate hit and counting the remaining hits before the cast window ends.

Partial coverage means the item projection includes only the modeled effects. Legend picks describe the evidence for the hero and role. Damage estimates describe the selected game scenario.
