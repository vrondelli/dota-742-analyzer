How these rankings work +

Gain per 1,000g divides modeled damage gained over the same hero without items by the build’s cost, then scales it to 1,000 gold. Each build belongs to one spend band and must improve on cheaper choices for that hero.

A Legend role leaderboard uses heroes assigned to that position in eligible replays. Patch membership may be inferred from the match start time. The full roster view compares all hero profiles without that position filter.

Magic models one cast of the strongest supported learned direct spell, with eligible item damage over the selected window. Exclude attack procs to compare casts without effects such as Monkey King Bar. Item-only results use a zero-spell baseline and have their own ranking.

Ability damage ranks gains per cast. Omnislash projects whole hits against one stationary target, starting with an immediate hit and counting the remaining hits before the cast window ends.

Partial coverage means the item projection includes only the modeled effects. Legend picks describe the evidence for the hero and role. Damage estimates describe the selected game scenario.
