How match results are ranked +

Hero rank uses the low end of a 95% Wilson interval for win rate, which accounts for sample size. Pick rate is each hero’s share of the picks covered by your filters.

Match rank uses the lobby's estimated average medal. Position and purchase information depends on available replays.

Item results compare first purchases in the selected window. The groups can differ in gold lead, draft, or game length.
