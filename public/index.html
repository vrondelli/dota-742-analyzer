How rankings work +

Hero ranks use the lower bound of a 95% Wilson interval for win rate. Pick rate is the share of eligible hero picks in the selected cohort.

Rank is estimated from the lobby's average medal. Role estimates come from available replays. Patch membership can be inferred from match start time.

Item results compare first purchases during the selected window with eligible games without that purchase in the same window. The win-rate difference is an observed association.
